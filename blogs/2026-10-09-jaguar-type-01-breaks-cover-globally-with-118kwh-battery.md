Jaguar has revealed the Type 01, a four-door electric grand tourer built on its new Jaguar Electric Architecture (JEA). It is the first production model under the brand's new design approach and is described as an electric luxury GT. With up to 1,030 PS, the Type 01 is the most powerful road car Jaguar has built so far.
