Kia India has expanded its Assured Buyback Programme across its petrol, diesel, CNG, hybrid and electric models. The announcement was made on October 9, 2026. Under the plan, eligible customers can get an assured residual value of up to 75% for ICE models and up to 70% for EVs after three years.
